# Coaching display guide

Version 31 · DRAFT. Numbered items are screen text; indented paragraphs are their optional Guide me guidance. Questions and tries are separate choices, not paired activities.

**Start.** From the Classic compass, tap the centre, then Coaching. Choose Progress, Stuck, Unclear, Explore, AI or Team. An opener is optional: tap the Coaching compass centre, then Another opener.

**Controls.** Paths changes context. Another cycles within the current path and question/try type. Try (optional) and Question switch types; each remembers its own place. Guide me opens the current guidance; Back returns to where you came from. For an opener, use Options > Guide me.

**Finishing and reminders.** Release opens the closing prompts. Options > Reminders opens the listening reminders. Next cycles within either group; Opening returns to the compass. Both screens also offer Back and Release, but no Guide me. Guide offers Back, Release, Opening and Reminders.

**Options.** The centre of the compass, or Options on an opener, opens the menu: Back, Another opener, Guide me, Release, Classic, Instant ON/OFF, Reminders and Sound ON/OFF. Classic changes mode; Instant skips the opening animation but not its reading hold; Sound controls the optional cue.

**Scrolling and motion.** Scroll when Scroll for full text appears. S arms a temporary shake request; the tap does not draw a card. Accepted shakes or flips draw an opener. Coaching automatic-shake reliability remains unresolved; use touch controls when needed.

**2.** What is working well that you would like to keep?

**3.** What's surprised you about your progress?

**4.** What would you like to build on next?

*Guide me for all questions in this section:*

> Make room for useful progress. Do not manufacture a problem or assume why something worked.

**3.** What's been the hardest part to move past?

**4.** What might you be assuming that hasn't been tested?

*Guide me for all questions in this section:*

## Team

### Questions

**1.** How is working together going for you?

**2.** Where do you see things differently?

**3.** Are there perspectives we haven't heard yet?

**4.** From your perspective, what matters most right now?

*Guide me for all questions in this section:*

> Differences can remain visible. Do not invent agreement or diagnose silence. Offer passing, writing, drawing, spoken or paired alternatives; sharing is optional.

### Tries

**1.** Would a quiet minute before sharing help?

> Listen for differences; do not diagnose silence or infer agreement from silence or sharing. Quiet thinking, writing or drawing is optional; offer spoken or paired alternatives. Passing needs no explanation. Sharing is voluntary; nothing needs collecting. Adapt, decline, request direct help or continue human-only. If checking a shared claim, AI/tools may use sources or observations; AI self-critique is not proof. Match evidence to consequences, not exhaustive human re-auditing.

**2.** Would it help to privately write, draw or discuss an uncertainty in pairs, then optionally share differences?

## Listening reminders

**1.** Stay curious. Listen before solving. Follow their words, not your assumptions.

**2.** Silence can be thinking time; do not diagnose it. Offer direct assistance when requested.

**3.** Passing, writing, drawing, speaking or pairs are legitimate choices. Let people own their next step.

**4.** Invite a brief round without interruptions. State that passing is welcome and private or paired contributions are alternatives. Reflect back ideas without attaching judgments to who spoke or stayed quiet.

## AI-RESULTS-Q02 — visible prompt

**Current main text:** What were you hoping to see instead?

**Draft example:** How does the output compare with what you hoped for?

**Reason:** Keeps expectation comparison while permitting useful, mixed or disappointing output. It does not assume a defect.

**Scope/tradeoff:** This is a local Coaching prompt, not a canonical text reference. Preserve the existing conditional private guidance and review it for consistency.

## AI-RESULTS-E02 — candidate private-guidance lead cue

**Current main text:** Would it help to check one claim together?

**Current private guidance:** Understand expected versus actual results before changing prompts. The goal, context, data, tool limits or choice to use AI may matter. Only if they report disappointing output, you could ask: What part felt least useful? This is optional: adapt, decline, request direct help, or choose another next step. AI self-critique is not proof; use evidence suited to the consequence, not exhaustive human auditing.

**Draft example:** Choose one consequential claim. Match it to a source, observation or executable check; keep it unresolved if evidence is unavailable.

**Reason:** Makes the basis for one claim check concrete without model agreement or mandatory exhaustive human checking.

**Scope/tradeoff:** A local guidance candidate, NOT a complete replacement. Retain permission to decline/adapt and the no-self-critique-as-proof safeguard. Editing tg-003.activity does not automatically update this invitation-based entry.

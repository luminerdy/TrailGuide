# Loop map and hypothetical walkthroughs

This is a desk analysis of existing materials, not a report of observed mentoring sessions, completed role-plays or learning outcomes. It proposes no required card sequence, new set of learner stages or additional card collection. Use the exact Define → Direct → Check → Adjust loop as a flexible aid; choose a checking plan during Define when appropriate.

## Six-path loop coverage

### progress

- **Define:** Q04 names the next useful extension; plan its sign of usefulness before E01.
- **Direct:** E02 optionally gives AI a challenger role; help style can come from the existing brief.
- **Check:** E02/E03 ask for evidence/observation, not model assurance.
- **Adjust:** Q02/Q04 preserve or build on a useful move; tg-026 supplies evidence limits.
- **ai evidence vs self critique:** AI may challenge an assumption in E02, but consequential claims require actual evidence; E03 seeks one observation. Success can remain a learner's purpose judgment, not a forced audit.
- **choice privacy and stakes:** All experiments allow adaptation/decline/direct help and reject exhaustive auditing. Privacy specifics are stronger in canonical tg-024 and PROJECT-COACH.md than in path text; apply before sharing, not after a trial. Use consequence-suited evidence; unavailable checks stay unresolved. No fixed number of trials or critics.

## Seven hypothetical situations

These are examples to inspect, not instructions to require fresh user role-plays. Existing completed observations should be reused where relevant. No participant identity, transcript or prompt collection is needed.

### Scenario 1

- **id:** SC01
- **status:** hypothetical desk-walkthrough; not an observed participant session
- **situation:** A new learner does not yet know what project they want.
- **existing resources:** OPEN-04; UNCLEAR-Q01; UNCLEAR-Q03; UNCLEAR-Q04; UNCLEAR-E01; docs/COACHING-DIRECTION.md
- **adaptation:** Ask what they would like to make, investigate or improve, or what curiosity matters. Let an explanation or a small example help them notice an interest; a one-sentence problem is optional, not proof they are ready.
- **can skip:** Skip discovery/surprise openers, technical tools and problem-statement writing if not useful. They can observe, pass or stop without picking a path.
- **residual gap:** The broader direction supplies the project-start invitation; a random discovery opener can misfit. A tiny bridge to the direction is more valuable than a new project-selection curriculum.
- **cheap observable:** The learner may name an interest, request an example or decline without accepting a prechosen goal.

### Scenario 2

- **id:** SC02
- **status:** hypothetical desk-walkthrough; not an observed participant session
- **situation:** A person asks for a demonstration of how to revise a paragraph or interpret a small table.
- **existing resources:** REMIND-02; OPEN-02; STUCK-Q01; tg-010; docs/PROJECT-COACH.md
- **adaptation:** Provide the requested worked example using suitable non-sensitive material. Establish what useful would look like and show the check: audience/purpose fit for the paragraph, or input/units/calculation for the table. Invite but do not demand that the learner try a part.
- **can skip:** Skip the two-question suggestion, further questioning and an unrequested quiz. No need to declare the person Stuck or use the device.
- **residual gap:** Demonstration is explicitly permitted in the project brief and canonical AI help, but is not prominent on the Coaching experiment faces. Improve retrieval, not a mandatory demonstration card.
- **cheap observable:** The requested demonstration actually appears and the learner can choose more explanation, practice, direct help or stopping.

### Scenario 3

- **id:** SC03
- **status:** hypothetical desk-walkthrough; not an observed participant session
- **situation:** A learner is making useful progress and likes the result.
- **existing resources:** PROGRESS-Q01; PROGRESS-Q02; PROGRESS-Q04; PROGRESS-E01; PROGRESS-E03; CLOSE-01
- **adaptation:** Recognize what they value. If useful, transfer the approach to another example or identify one confidence-strengthening observation. Keep an account of why it worked tentative.
- **can skip:** Skip AI challenge E02 and any experiment when no investigation is wanted. Release can occur after listening.
- **residual gap:** No substantive lack of a positive-progress route. The opportunity is to retain this strength rather than funnel all sessions into fault-finding.
- **cheap observable:** The chosen next action can preserve success, continue work or stop; a newly invented problem is not required.

### Scenario 4

- **id:** SC04
- **status:** hypothetical desk-walkthrough; not an observed participant session
- **situation:** An AI research answer includes a plausible but false factual claim.
- **existing resources:** AI-RESULTS-Q01; AI-RESULTS-E01; AI-RESULTS-E02; tg-003; tg-021; tg-023
- **adaptation:** Choose the claim that matters for intended use. Ask AI/tools to locate the original passage or execute a suitable calculation, inspect what the evidence actually establishes, and correct or leave unverified. A criticism of the answer also needs support before changing the work.
- **can skip:** Skip another model as a ritual, exhaustive checking of harmless details and prompt tweaks unrelated to the cause. If evidence is inaccessible, do not use the claim to justify a risky action.
- **residual gap:** The source-backed route already exists. Newer facilitators may need a concrete example of choosing a check and stopping once adequate evidence is available.
- **cheap observable:** The claim is supported, contradicted or explicitly unresolved on actual evidence, not merely labeled reviewed by AI.

### Scenario 5

- **id:** SC05
- **status:** hypothetical desk-walkthrough; not an observed participant session
- **situation:** AI is unavailable, not useful, or the learner prefers human-only work.
- **existing resources:** EXPLORING-E01; EXPLORING-Q04; STUCK-E02; tg-014; tg-030; CLOSE-01
- **adaptation:** Sketch a human route or use paper/spoken sorting, then select a useful small step. If comparison matters, include instruction, checking, correction, integration and learning effort; human assistance is also a legitimate choice.
- **can skip:** Skip AI comparison entirely; neither AI adoption nor completing a test is required to finish.
- **residual gap:** No missing permission for human-only work. Canonical total-effort comparison is richer than Coaching's short invitation; use its bridge sparingly.
- **cheap observable:** The person continues or stops on their chosen route without having to justify refusal or demonstrate AI use.

### Scenario 6

- **id:** SC06
- **status:** hypothetical desk-walkthrough; not an observed participant session
- **situation:** A quiet team includes a senior decision-maker and a learner who may not want to disagree publicly.
- **existing resources:** TEAM-Q01; TEAM-Q02; TEAM-E01; TEAM-E02; TEAM-E03; REMIND-02; REMIND-03; tg-033; tg-035; tg-039
- **adaptation:** Offer private reflection, solo/pair alternatives or passing; do not infer fear, consent or agreement from silence. Do not automatically collect private notes or share a partner's contribution. Keep dissent visible only as safely and voluntarily offered. Roles/owners require agreement, not the senior person's assumption.
- **can skip:** Skip public rounds, pair reporting or AI synthesis if those would add pressure or expose private content. One individual conversation or no collection may be more appropriate.
- **residual gap:** Text supports non-diagnostic inclusion but does not itself remedy authority relations. A short conditional boundary is warranted; no safety guarantee or diagnosis follows from this walkthrough.
- **cheap observable:** The proposed summary contains only voluntarily shared content and explicit decisions; someone may pass without explanation and without their quietness being interpreted.

### Scenario 7

- **id:** SC07
- **status:** hypothetical desk-walkthrough; not an observed participant session
- **situation:** The learner wants to continue alone next week, possibly with another AI or a human specialist.
- **existing resources:** CLOSE-01; CLOSE-03; PROGRESS-E01; tg-007; tg-011; tg-026; tg-040; docs/PROJECT-COACH.md
- **adaptation:** Offer only useful brief fields: present goal/state, next step, wanted help, evidence and open uncertainty. If independence is wanted, invite them to lead Define → Direct → Check → Adjust while the mentor reduces prompts. Include when a human specialist, different tool or pause would be sensible.
- **can skip:** Skip written capture, AI use, a demonstration of mastery and mandatory reflection. A short spoken plan can suffice.
- **residual gap:** The existing brief is substantial; the short Closing face does not surface it. One portable plan does not prove durable learning, and no card-use telemetry can substitute for that evidence.
- **cheap observable:** Given the context they chose to keep, they can select an action and a proportionate check or appropriate help without depending on the original facilitator to reconstruct it.

## Illustrative breadth — not coverage quotas

- **context:** writing
- **current anchors:** tg-010; tg-016; AI-RESULTS-E03
- **illustrative adaptation:** Revise a short explanation using intended audience, purpose and two useful qualities; AI may demonstrate or collaborate.
- **checking basis:** Compare with source facts and agreed audience/purpose constraints; human judgment for voice and usefulness.
- **limit:** Polish is not evidence of factual support or learning.

- **context:** research
- **current anchors:** UNCLEAR-Q04; tg-009; tg-021; tg-034
- **illustrative adaptation:** Narrow a curiosity, trace consequential claims to original passages and retain missing perspectives.
- **checking basis:** Source existence, exact support, context/applicability and unresolved contradictions.
- **limit:** AI role-play is not stakeholder evidence.

- **context:** code
- **current anchors:** tg-004; tg-003; tg-019; tg-038
- **illustrative adaptation:** Choose explanation, pairing or implementation for one bounded behavior; use authorized checks.
- **checking basis:** Executed tests/real behavior, scope, dependencies and observable limits.
- **limit:** Proposed commands or another agent's critique are not executed evidence.

- **context:** data/numerical work
- **current anchors:** tg-023; tg-019; AI-RESULTS-E01
- **illustrative adaptation:** Interpret a small sanitized table, trace an input and recalculate a consequential result.
- **checking basis:** Input provenance, units, actual calculator/code execution and awkward/missing examples.
- **limit:** Matching arithmetic does not validate unreliable inputs or a broader inference.

- **context:** creative work
- **current anchors:** tg-013; tg-010; PROGRESS-E01; EXPLORING-E02
- **illustrative adaptation:** Compare two story openings or borrow a pattern from another field while preserving chosen qualities.
- **checking basis:** Learner-owned intent, constraints, representative examples and human judgment; check factual claims only where consequential.
- **limit:** Do not impose a universal winner, factual checklist or simulated audience approval.

- **context:** decision/planning
- **current anchors:** UNCLEAR-E03; tg-036; tg-037; tg-038; tg-040
- **illustrative adaptation:** Compare a human, AI-assisted or hybrid route for the next milestone with criteria and real constraints.
- **checking basis:** Available evidence, full effort, authority, reversibility and a reconsideration trigger.
- **limit:** Unknowns stay visible; no invented scores or assumed consensus.

- **context:** teamwork
- **current anchors:** TEAM-E02; TEAM-E03; tg-031; tg-033; tg-035; tg-039
- **illustrative adaptation:** Offer accessible contribution modes and temporary voluntary roles, then summarize explicit next actions.
- **checking basis:** Voluntarily shared contributions and explicit permission/agreement; no inference from silence.
- **limit:** Participation formats do not guarantee safety under power asymmetry.

## Proportionate evaluation plan

- **purpose:** Inspect content usability and learner-owned continuation, not prove learning effectiveness.
- **initial validation:** Use the seven documented desk-walkthroughs and existing source/render evidence first. These are hypothetical, not new role-play observations or a requirement for further user testing.
- **optional next evidence:** If an actual mentoring opportunity arises with permission, choose one meaningful milestone and one relevant uncertainty from the backlog. Use existing completed observations where applicable; do not reopen an exhaustive review.
- **look for:** A learner-chosen next useful result and a proportionate checking basis, preferably before generation.; Requested explanation/demonstration/direct help is provided rather than withheld for a question quota.; AI/tools can assist the check, with important claims tied to real evidence; uncertainty stays visible.; Human-only work, passing, adaptation and release without experiment remain complete choices.; A chosen route considers correction/integration/learning effort when that choice matters.; A learner can retain a useful next step and choose later human/AI help; distinguish supported performance from independent continuation.
- **privacy:** No required transcripts, participant identities, prompt collection or device logs. Use fictional/sanitized artifacts and voluntary feedback; do not infer consent or outcomes from silence or clicks.
- **stop rule:** Resolve one material content uncertainty and revise only the affected support. Do not expand cards merely to fill the heuristic context map.
- **claim limits:** No psychometric scores, numerical effectiveness claims, certification, durable-learning inference from one session, or hardware acceptance from this content review.

# Coaching content-review companion files

- `COACHING-CARD-AUDIT.csv`: all55 Coaching entries. Exact current text/guidance are separate from reviewer notes and unapproved examples. Blank guidance means no separate field exists, not that a field was lost.
- `CANONICAL-CARD-AUDIT.csv`: all40 canonical cards, their12-card Classic selection and Coaching consumers.
- `SHARED-TEXT-CROSSWALK.csv`: all11 imported fields and their change consequences.
- `DRAFT-EXAMPLES.md`: six selectively curated illustrations; no source edits or complete-guide replacement.
- `LOOP-AND-SCENARIO-MAP.md`: six-path analysis and seven hypothetical walkthroughs, not performed sessions.

All CSVs use UTF-8 with a BOM for common spreadsheet readers. Dispositions mean keep/refine/clarify/discuss, not numerical quality scores or editorial approval. Suggested validations are unrun ideas, not requirements to conduct new role-plays or exhaustive trials. No wording, review state, firmware, device or publication changed.
